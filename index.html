About the event

What even is this?

Built with Nature is a makeathon where human creativity and the natural world blend together, empowering creators to shape organic materials into visual art.

The event brings together artists, designers, and makers to focus entirely on creative expression and visual storytelling.

This event is not focused on solving problems — it is a pure celebration of tactile creativity, visual storytelling, and material exploration.

Participants can create organic sculptures, illustrations, wearable art, and art installations, or anything from their imagination, using only natural materials.

The ground rules

The Golden Rules

01

Use Natural Materials

Create using leaves, twigs, stones, seeds, soil, and other natural materials.

02

Let Creativity Flow

No fixed formats. Sculpt, paint, build, or experiment with your imagination.

03

Teams of 1–2

Create solo or team up with a fellow artist or maker.

04

Create During the Event

Bring your ideas to life on the day. No pre-made creations allowed.

05

Showcase Your Creation

Present your artwork and share the story behind it.

06

Respect Nature

Create responsibly. Use materials sustainably and leave nature unharmed.
